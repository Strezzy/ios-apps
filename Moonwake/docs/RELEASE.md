# Moonwake 1.0 — The first journey

A native Swift pixel-art adventure beneath a broken moon. Play as Aster, with his glowing starlight sword, amber spirit Wisp and moss-covered guardian companion.

Explore five connected realms, awaken fifteen runes, face five guardians, restore the moonwells and reach the ending. Find hidden caches, earn stardust, upgrade your sword and abilities, and save your journey locally. Includes animated pixel environments, original ambient music and combat sounds, touch controls, controller/keyboard input and native Liquid Glass menus.

**Install:** download `Moonwake.ipa` and import it into your existing sideloading setup. Requires iOS 26 or later; landscape iPhone and iPad. The IPA must be re-signed by your installer.

**Play:** left thumbstick to move, sword to strike, Dash to evade, Moonpulse for area magic. Use the nearby interaction prompt for runes, caches, Moss and moonwells. Your journal explains the current objective. Pause to upgrade, adjust controls or switch to Story difficulty.

**Validation:** the attached build and test reports record the exact source commit and automated results. Physical iPhone performance, heat, battery use, haptics, hardware controllers and sideload-host behavior remain to be validated on device.

The game runs offline and has no accounts, advertisements, purchases or analytics.
