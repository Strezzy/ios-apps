Cask 3 — Scrolling Animations

Scrolling animations adapted from Cask 3 by Ryan Nair (@ryannair05), inspired by
the original Cask by Ryan Petrich. Thank you both for bringing personality to
scrolling.

Project: https://github.com/ryannair05/Cask-3
Original Cask creator: https://github.com/rpetrich

Music includes an Objective-C adaptation of the animation presets in the
upstream Sources/Cask.swift file. The original authors are credited for their
work; this Music adaptation is maintained separately.

Changes for Music: the presets animate app-owned content inside Music's rows
using public UIKit and Core Animation APIs. Row reuse, scrolling, interaction,
settings, and accessibility control when effects start and stop. The adaptation
keeps the row model geometry stable, isolates existing swipe and glass controls,
corrects the Rotate angle and Shake keyframe timing, and bounds spring and color
effects for readable app content. It does not include Cask 3's system-wide hooks,
injection code, or jailbreak preferences.

An unmodified copy of the complete Apache 2.0 license is included alongside this
notice as Cask3-LICENSE.txt and can be read in Music's scrolling-animation
credits. The source download also includes the unmodified upstream animation
file, README, license, and pinned source provenance under Support/Upstream/Cask3.
