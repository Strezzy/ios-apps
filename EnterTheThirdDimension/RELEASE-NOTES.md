# Enter the Third Dimension 2.1 (3)

A raindrop falls from a tree and learns to take the shape of the lives it
touches. Play as a smooth, reflective water girl, frog, or fox in the mystical
rainforest of **The Folded Forest**.

## New in this build

- A new opening sequence follows the drop from the tree to the ground and
  through her first transformation into a human shape after a gentle touch.
- The player is a smooth, translucent water girl with bright reflections,
  moving surface highlights, and elastic character motion.
- Touch the moss frog and forest fox to remember their shapes, then change
  between your learned water forms with the Form button.
- The entire forest now uses smooth geometry: curved roots, broad leaves,
  ferns, rounded mossy ledges, flowers, glowing mushrooms, and cute chibi
  inhabitants. The earlier pixel scenery and paper houses are replaced.
- Enter the third dimension through a new transformation: the girl floats,
  gathers into a drop, and reforms with volume while the world turns.
- Other inhabitants and magical leaf obstacles remain flat. Step around the first wall
  and explore a hidden bridge behind the broken road.
- Play Chapter I, **The Folded Forest**: find the prism, recover a lost spark,
  light the village gate, and collect up to twelve optional shards.
- The left/right touch pad unfolds into a depth thumbstick. Jump and movement
  work together, with adjustable jump height, forgiving edge jumps, and
  lantern checkpoints.
- Forest geometry, original character animation, moonlight, aurora,
  fireflies, restrained glow and ripple effects, and original synthesized sound.
- Pause, reduced-effects and sound settings, local saved progress, and safe
  return-to-2D checks that prevent folding into obstacles or unsupported space.

## Build and graphics

Compiled using Apple's actual SDK and the established UIKit scene/window
launch flow. Publication requires an actual native simulator launch, a
completed WebGL frame, a successfully rendered water character, and verified
arm64 iPhone packaging. Build evidence
and checksums are attached to this release. Physical iPhone playtesting remains
the next validation step.

All game art and shaders in this build are original. The suggested Shaders
repository was reviewed and is MIT licensed; its WebGPU runtime and separately
licensed website presets are not included. The game runs entirely offline.
