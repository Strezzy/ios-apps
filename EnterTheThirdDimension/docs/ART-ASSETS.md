# Raindrop update: artwork and assets

## Game graphics

The forest, inhabitants, water shapes, surface materials, and transitions are
generated by original runtime code in `Resources/Game/rainforest.js`,
`Resources/Game/water.js`, and `Resources/Game/render.js`. No character models,
texture packs, or shader presets are downloaded during play.

The current remembered forms are the girl, frog, and fox. This is an explicit
roster: adding a future creature involves adding its normal and water shapes,
world placement, and movement settings. The game does not generate arbitrary
new creature models at runtime.

The liquid appearance uses refraction from a captured scene texture, stylized
environment reflections, surface highlights, and deformed geometry. Movement
drives damped springs for squash and lean. These are interactive optical and
elastic effects; they are not a computational fluid simulation.

## App icon

The icon was created with the built-in image-generation tool for this release.
It is promotional icon artwork, not a screenshot of the running game.

- Source used by the Xcode icon catalog: `Resources/RaindropIcon1024.png`.
- Public app catalog asset: `../assets/ThirdDimension-Rain.png`.
- Export: opaque, 1024 × 1024 RGB PNG; the generated square was resized for
  the required icon dimensions. The previous icon remains in source history.

### Generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished iOS app icon for Enter the
> Third Dimension, a mystical rainforest game about a living raindrop that
> remembers the forms of creatures it touches. Create one beautiful square
> 1024 by 1024 opaque PNG, full bleed, no rounded corners baked into the image.
> Primary subject: a single friendly translucent aquamarine raindrop, plump and
> softly jelly-like, resting delicately in a curved rainforest leaf. Two tiny
> dark expressive eyes and tiny warm smile; expressive but understated. It
> should feel like an exquisitely rendered living water spirit. Smooth 3D
> material with crystal-clear refraction, pearly Fresnel edges, a bold glossy
> moon highlight, delicate iridescence and a faint inner glow. A simple deep
> teal and midnight-blue rainforest atmosphere behind it with a few softly
> blurred luminous leaves and two tiny golden fireflies. Large central
> silhouette, legible even as a small phone icon. Sophisticated magical, cute,
> tactile, beautifully lit. No pixels or voxel style, no text, no letters, no
> frame, no watermark, no additional characters. Preserve true
> transparent-looking water material against the opaque background; image
> itself must be completely opaque.

## Preview evidence

The build's `Simulator-Launch.png` is an actual native iOS Simulator capture.
The QA report identifies the browser/runtime and distinguishes synthetic
touch checks from physical iPhone testing. No generated artwork is used as
gameplay validation evidence.
